What it does

Cancels you can still turn around.

Most cancels have a reason you can fix. Vantly asks, offers the fix, and only cancels if the customer still wants to.

Reasons per product

Boards, boots, or anything else each get their own questions and offers, so the customer sees choices that make sense for what they bought.

Offers that fix the problem

Suggest a different product, a discount, a size swap, faster shipping, or an address fix, matched to the reason they gave.

Never ships mid-decision

The order is paused while the customer decides, and released to fulfillment if they keep it.

Know why people cancel

Reasons, answers, and revenue saved, in one report you can act on.
