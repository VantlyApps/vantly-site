Frequently asked questions

When is Vantly Discounts available?

It's in development and coming soon to the Shopify App Store. Email support@vantlyapps.com to hear when it launches.

Can one discount have several offers?

Yes. That's the point: one discount holds many proposals, checked from the top, for products, the order, and shipping.

Can an item get two proposals?

No. Each item gets at most one product proposal: the first one it fits.

Can I discount only the cheapest items?

Yes. In the proposal, under Discount these items, set How many to a number and the cheapest ones are discounted.

Does it work with codes and automatic discounts?

Yes, either one. You can switch later.

Can I test a discount before customers see it?

Yes. Turn on Test mode and only customers tagged test get it.

Does it stack with my other discounts?

Only if you allow it under Combines with, on this discount and the other one.

Does it work with subscriptions?

Yes. Choose all recurring payments or the first payment only.

How do I set up buy one, get one free?

Make a cart group for the product. Add a product proposal: under When the cart has, Quantity at least 1 in that group; under Discount these items, How many 1 in the same group; Percentage 100; and turn on Use up the matched items. It repeats for each pair: 2 in the cart gets 1 free, 4 gets 2 free.

How do I set up buy 3, get 1 at 50% off?

Same as buy one, get one: Quantity at least 3 in the group, How many 1 in the same group, Percentage 50, and Use up the matched items on. 4 in the cart gets 1 at half price, 8 gets 2. The cheapest one is the discounted one.

Is there a limit on how big a discount can be?

Yes, Shopify's. Shopify passes at most 10,000 bytes of settings per discount to checkout: about 590 hand-picked products, or about 45 proposals. The Discount size meter under Summary shows how full it is, and warns when it gets close. If it gets too big, Vantly won't send it to Shopify; use collections or tags instead of long product lists, or split it into two discounts.

Why does Summary say the discount works on carts up to a number of items?

Every collection and tag a discount checks is sent along with every item in the cart, and Shopify caps how much it sends. Checking many collections or tags lowers the biggest cart the discount still works on. Check fewer collections or tags, or combine them into one.

What does the Free plan include?

Every feature, with one discount turned on at a time (live or scheduled). A discount you add while one is already on is saved but stays off, marked Needs attention, until you upgrade or end the other one. Discounts that are already on keep working.

How do I upgrade to Pro?

Open Settings > Plan and billing and click Upgrade to Pro. Shopify shows its plan page; Pro starts with a 14-day free trial and is billed through your Shopify invoice. Pro runs as many discounts as you like.

Does it slow down checkout?

No. The discount runs inside Shopify's checkout as a Shopify Function; it never waits on Vantly's servers.
