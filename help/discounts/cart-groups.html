Cart groups

Group products by collection, tag, metafield or price, and reuse them.

A cart group is a named set of products that proposals check and discount, like "Boards" or "Bindings". Only you see the name.

What can put an item in a group

  • Product, Collection, or Product tag (is or is not).
  • Product or variant metafield equals or doesn't equal a value.
  • Line attribute equals or doesn't equal a value.
  • Line quantity or Item price is at least an amount.
  • Purchase type: one-time or subscription.
  • Compare-at price: set (already on sale) or none (not already on sale).

Order matters

Groups are checked from the top. An item joins only the first group it fits, unless you turn on An item can join every group it fits. All always holds every item in the cart.

Limits

One discount can use up to 10 different collections and 10 different product tags across its groups. Groups that use a metafield or line attribute need its key under Advanced on the discount; Shopify allows one of each per discount.
