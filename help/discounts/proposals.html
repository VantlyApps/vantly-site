Proposals

When the cart has…, discount these lines…, by: amount, percentage or fixed price.

A proposal is one offer inside a discount. It has two parts.

1. When the cart has

The conditions the cart must meet. Pick a Measure (Quantity, Total, Unique products, Unique variants, or Unique groups), at least or at most, an amount, and the group to count In group. Add more with And another condition. With no conditions, it applies to every cart.

2. Discount these items

(Product proposals only.) Pick the group to discount, or All (every item), and How many: leave it at All, or enter a number to discount only the cheapest ones. Add another group discounts items in more groups.

3. By

Pick a Discount type:

  • Amount: money off each item, for example $50 off.
  • Percentage: percent off each item, for example 25% off.
  • Fixed price: the item sells for a set price, for example $149.

On an order proposal, Amount and Percentage come off the order subtotal (Fixed price isn't offered). On a shipping proposal, Amount comes off each shipping option, Percentage off shipping (100% makes it free), and Fixed price sets what shipping costs, for example $4.99.

Turn on Apply to compare-at price to take the discount off the higher compare-at price instead of today's price (never more than today's price).

Use up the matched items

With Use up the matched items on, items counted toward this proposal can't count toward later ones, and with a number in How many, the offer repeats for every full set in the cart (for example, buy 2 boards and get 1 binding free, for each set).

Currency

A proposal works in All currencies by default (amounts are converted from your store currency), or pick one currency so it only applies to carts in that currency.
