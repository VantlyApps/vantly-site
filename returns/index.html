Returns

Every return, handled the way your store would.

Set it up once. Vantly follows your policy on every return, and your team only steps in when it matters.

Rules that read like your policy

Tags, collections, SKUs, order discounts, customer history, item condition. Test any rule against a real order before it goes live.

if product tag is snowboard and condition is opened then 15% restocking fee

Exchanges before refunds

Instant exchanges, bonus store credit, and the customer's original discount carried over. If the new item costs more, they get an invoice for the difference.

Size 9→Size 10+ LABOR discount kept

Labels at your rates

USPS, UPS, FedEx, DHL and more, through your ShipStation or EasyPost account. Vantly compares them and buys the cheapest or fastest label automatically.

Green returns

When shipping it back costs more than it's worth, the customer keeps it. They get a friendly email, not a label.

Restock the right SKU

Opened items can go back as a different product, like new to open-box, in Shopify or Katana.
