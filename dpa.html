Data Processing Agreement

Vantly Returns · Last updated September 30, 2026

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") applies between the merchant that installs Vantly Returns ("Merchant") and Vantly ("Vantly", "we") whenever Vantly processes personal data for the Merchant. By installing and using the app, the Merchant agrees to this DPA. It supplements the Shopify terms that govern apps and our Privacy Policy.

1. Roles

The Merchant is the controller of its customers' personal data. Vantly is a processor that handles that data only on the Merchant's behalf and on its instructions. Using and configuring the app counts as those instructions.

2. What we process

  • Who: the Merchant's customers who start a return, and the Merchant's staff who use the app.
  • What: names, email addresses, phone numbers, shipping addresses, order and item details, return reasons, notes, and photos customers upload. Staff names, emails, and roles.
  • Why: to provide the returns service: looking up orders, processing returns, refunds, store credit, and exchanges, creating shipping labels, restocking, sending return emails, and reporting.
  • How long: for as long as the Merchant uses the app, subject to the retention periods in our Privacy Policy.

3. Our commitments

  • We process personal data only for the purposes above and never sell it or use it for advertising.
  • Anyone at Vantly with access to personal data is bound by confidentiality.
  • We maintain the security measures described on our Security page, including encryption in transit and at rest, access control, and access logging.
  • We help the Merchant respond to customer requests to access or delete their data. Deletion requests sent through Shopify are carried out automatically.
  • We tell the Merchant without undue delay, and within 72 hours of confirming it, about any breach affecting the Merchant's personal data, and we help the Merchant with its own notification duties.
  • When the Merchant uninstalls the app, we delete the Merchant's data after Shopify's deletion request, which Shopify sends 48 hours after uninstall. Backups age out on the database provider's schedule.

4. Sub-processors

The Merchant authorizes the service providers listed on our Security page. We require each to protect personal data at least as well as this DPA does. We'll update that list before adding a new sub-processor that handles customer personal data, and the Merchant may object by contacting us or uninstall the app.

5. Transfers

Data is stored and processed in the United States. Where the law requires a transfer mechanism, such as for data from the EU, UK, or Switzerland, the parties rely on the transfer terms that apply to the Merchant's use of Shopify apps, or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, which the parties agree to where needed.

6. Information and audits

On reasonable request, we'll give the Merchant the information needed to show we meet this DPA, for example by answering a security questionnaire.

7. General

If this DPA conflicts with other terms between us about personal data, this DPA wins. We may update it to reflect changes in law or the service, and will note the date above. Questions: privacy@vantlyapps.com.
