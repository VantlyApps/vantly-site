Vantly Returns

A branded return portal that turns refunds into exchanges and store credit, buys the label, and restocks the right SKU.

  • Exchanges first. Instant exchanges and bonus credit before a refund.
  • Labels at your rates. Through your ShipStation or EasyPost account.
  • Package protection. Offered in the cart, and you keep 100% of the fee.

Vantly Cancellations

A self-serve cancel page for unshipped orders. Customers say why, see an offer made for that reason, and can still cancel in a couple of taps.

  • Offers that fix the problem. A size swap, a discount, faster shipping, an address fix.
  • Never ships mid-decision. The order is paused while the customer decides.
  • Know why people cancel. Reasons and revenue saved in one report.
